Why sell the car on Carmall24

It is not a website for posting new shipment notices, but moving your entire workflow from receiving inquiries to issuing sales orders online.

Direct Connection with Overseas Buyers

Buyers from markets such as Nigeria, Kenya, Saudi Arabia, and Russia post purchase requests directly on the platform; inquiries land in your backend — no need to rely on trade shows or intermediaries.

Per-VIN Quoting

Each used car has its own condition and price. Quotes are generated precisely by VIN, and vehicle details are automatically created upon a deal, so there's no need to match cars manually in Excel.

You Set the Price

Simply enter the tax-included vehicle price and gross margin; FOB / CIF prices are converted automatically. Inland transport, customs declaration, port charges, and ocean freight can be covered item by item.

Straight-Through ERP Integration

Quoting, negotiation, deal confirmation, and sales orders run in one chain. The transaction price is locked to the vehicle source; documents are never lost, and reconciliation is hassle-free.

Entry process, 4 steps

Submit online, manual review, available as soon as the same day.

  1. Step 1

    Submit enterprise information

    Fill in the enterprise name, unified social credit code, contact person and business type.

  2. Step 2

    Upload qualifications

    Business license, export qualification certificate and legal representative identity document. PDF files or images are both acceptable.

  3. Step 3

    Platform review

    Material verification will be completed within 1–2 working days, and the settlement agreement will be signed upon approval.

  4. Step 4

    List cars on the platform for sale

    Start receiving purchase inquiries, issuing quotations, negotiating prices, and synchronizing completed orders to your ERP.
